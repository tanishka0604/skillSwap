Why SkillSwap

Every skill you have is worth something to someone else

Most people know something worth teaching, and want to learn something they can't afford lessons for. SkillSwap treats knowledge as currency: instead of paying for a class, you pay with a class of your own.

You keep your profile simple — list what you can teach and what you're hoping to learn. We handle the part that's usually hardest: finding the person on the other side of that trade.

  • List, don't sell Add the skills you can teach — no pricing, no listings to manage.
  • Matched both ways You only see people who both teach what you want and want what you teach.
  • Talk before you commit Send a request, chat, and agree on how the swap will work.

How it works

From profile to swap in three steps

No algorithms to guess at — just a clear path from signing up to sitting down with someone to learn.

01

List your skills

Add what you can teach and what you want to learn. Be as specific as "React fundamentals" or as broad as "conversational French."

02

Find your match

SkillSwap looks for people whose "teach" list matches your "want" list — and whose "want" list matches yours.

03

Send a request and swap

Reach out, agree on a schedule, and start trading sessions. Chat stays open for the whole swap.
